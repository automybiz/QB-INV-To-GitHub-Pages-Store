1. Why Distributors Like Turn14 Are Paranoid About "AI" ▼

Turn14's business is selling physical boxes of parts, not giving away free data infrastructure. When an IT rep hears "AI", they worry about three specific threats:

  1. Wholesale Price & Margin Leakage:
    • Distributors protect their dealer wholesale buy tiers furiously. They worry an AI tool will scrape confidential jobber rates and leak them publicly.
  2. Catalog Scraping Without Buying Parts:
    • Many tech startups try to get API keys to scrape 500,000 SKUs to train public search models without spending a single dollar on inventory.
  3. Server Hammering & DDoS:
    • Unchecked automated bots can hammer their servers with thousands of simultaneous queries, degrading performance for real dealer order desks.

    3. It frames the connection as an "internal CRM/estimating integration" (completely standard and boring to IT compliance).
3. What to Do If They Pin You Down: "Are You Using AI?" ▼

If a rep or IT auditor point-blank asks: "Are you connecting this to AI or an LLM?", do not panic, stammer, or sound guilty. De-mystify it immediately by framing it as a natural-language search UI for your shop staff:

"Yes, our internal workflow software uses a private natural language search interface so our service advisors can type what they need (e.g. '2024 Porsche 911 water pump') rather than manually hunting through drop-down menus. 

It is strictly an internal, password-protected tool for our shop staff. It only makes on-demand single queries when a customer is standing at our counter or requesting an estimate. We do not mass scrape data, no public models are trained, and our sole purpose is to quote accurate Turn14 parts and order inventory from Turn14 for our shop's repair jobs."
  1. Key De-escalation Points in That Response:
    • "Strictly internal & password-protected": Eliminates public leak fears.
    • "On-demand single queries for customer estimates": Eliminates mass scraping / server abuse fears.
    • "To order inventory from Turn14": Reassures them that real revenue is coming.
4. The Golden Rule: Orders Trump Everything ▼

In wholesale distribution, accounts are judged on one metric: Purchase Volume ($$$).

  • How Distributors Treat Active Accounts:
    1. If an account queries the API 1,000 times a month and spends $0, they will shut the API key off.
    2. If an account queries the API 100 times a week and places $15,000 in monthly parts orders, Turn14 will give you any API endpoint, dedicated support, and higher rate limits with zero questions asked.
